About the Artist

Precious Blood was formed in 2020 by AJ (Jesus Knevil, Snake Canyon) and Grace Perez (The Wickedexorcist). Dave Kramer (Blood Feast, Suaka) joined in 2024.

The project combines their love for all things metal and the Caribbean into one unholy concoction of rum soaked riffs, brutal lyrics and mystical tribal sounds.

Precious Blood is ready to bring their death and thrash metal influenced sound, baptized with reggae and ska to the masses.
